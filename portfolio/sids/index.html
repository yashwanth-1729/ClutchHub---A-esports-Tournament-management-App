SIDS — Server-Independent Data Sync

A darkmatter from Voidbyte: it holds everything together while staying invisible. Your devices share one locked notebook through storage that can’t open it. No server, no accounts, no database.

How it works

One recovery code is the whole account: Argon2id at 64 MiB turns it into two keys, one to encrypt and one to sign. Every note is sealed with AES-256-GCM and padded to size buckets before it leaves the device. One HMAC-signed, numbered, hash-chained manifest lists what exists, so tampering and replay are hard stops. Writes are compare-and-swap, with backoff from 300 ms to 8 s. Clashes settle by revision, then device id, never by the clock; deletes are kept as tombstones.

Proof

92 assertions across eight test files, 15 more for the stateless write relay, and 8 of 8 checks passing live against the real GitHub API. Designed and built by Yashwanth Raj Cherukuru for the open-source edition of JARVIS.
